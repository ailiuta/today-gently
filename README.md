# Today, gently

A small daily planner: choose up to three priorities, park other tasks for another day, and check off what you finish. It saves your list in the browser on your device, needs no account, and caches the app for offline use after its first visit.

## Use on a phone or laptop

The app needs to be served over HTTPS (or localhost) for installation and offline support. To publish it free with GitHub Pages, upload these files to a repository, then enable Pages in **Settings → Pages** and choose the `main` branch and `/ (root)`. Open the resulting Pages address on your laptop or phone. On a phone, use the browser's **Add to Home Screen** or **Install app** option. On a laptop, choose **Install app** from the browser menu/address bar. The same URL works on both devices, but each device keeps its own private list.

You can also open `index.html` directly to use it in a browser, though installing and offline caching require HTTPS or localhost.

## Privacy

Tasks are stored in that browser's local storage. They are not synced between devices or sent to a server. Clearing browser data removes them.
